# Script Writing

The most important skill for YouTube success isn't editing, camera quality, or audio. It's keeping people watching. A video nobody finishes gets buried. A video people watch completely gets pushed to more people.

Two frameworks do the heavy lifting: the Tension Engine (structure) and the Combination Method (topic selection).

---

## The Tension Engine

Every video with 50%+ average view duration uses this pattern - usually without realizing it.

**Core principle:** Every 60–90 seconds, introduce information the viewer desperately wants resolved. Don't resolve it immediately. Add context. Then resolve it. Immediately open the next loop.

```
Open tension → Add context → Resolve → Open next tension → ...
```

The moment you close a loop without opening a new one, viewers leave.

### Tension Point Types

| Type | Example |
|------|---------|
| **Revelation** | "What I'm about to show you is the reason 95% of channels fail - and nobody talks about it" |
| **Contradiction** | "The advice everyone gives about thumbnails is actually backwards" |
| **Stakes** | "If you skip this step, none of the rest of this video matters" |
| **Cliffhanger** | "Before I show you the solution, you need to understand why the obvious answer doesn't work" |
| **Pattern break** | Introduce a concept, then say "but there's a catch" before explaining it |

### Building a Tension Engine Script

Structure every video like this:

```
[Hook - 0:00–0:30]
  Open strongest tension point immediately.
  State the payoff: what they'll know by the end.
  
[Loop 1 - 0:30–2:00]
  Introduce concept A → partial reveal → "but first..."
  Build context. Resolve A. Open B.

[Loop 2 - 2:00–4:00]
  Develop B → create sub-tension → resolve → open C.

[Loops 3–N]
  Repeat. Each resolution opens the next loop immediately.

[Final Loop → Pillar Video CTA]
  Last loop leads naturally into your pillar video.
  "Everything I just covered is the foundation. The full system is in [video]."
```

### The Hook Formula

First 30 seconds determine if people watch at all. Use:

1. **State the payoff** - tell them exactly what they'll get
2. **Challenge the assumption** - contradict what they think they know
3. **Create urgency** - why this matters now

Example:
> "You're about to see why everything you've been told about growing on YouTube is wrong - and the three-step framework that actually works. But first, you need to understand something that 99% of creators completely miss."

## The Combination Method

Don't guess at video ideas. Don't copy what's already been done. Combine two independently successful ideas into one new idea.

**Process:**
1. List 5–10 channels in your niche
2. Find videos with high view counts (especially from small channels - proof the topic, not the channel, drove views)
3. Make a list of those winning topics
4. Find two topics that seem unrelated or even contradictory
5. Combine them into one video concept

**Example:**
- Video A did well: "Side Hustles That Are Illegal"
- Video B did well: "YouTube Niches to Avoid"
- Combined: "YouTube Niches That Will Get Your Channel Banned"

The combination feels fresh because nobody's done it. But both halves are proven - you know audiences respond to each piece separately.

---

## The Information DJ Method

Once you have a topic, build the best video on that topic - not an original one. Remix what's already working.

**Process:**
1. Find the top 3 videos on your exact topic
2. Watch them at 2x speed
3. Bullet-point every point they make
4. Read the comments - find what viewers wished was covered
5. Include all of it in your script, plus the missing pieces from comments
6. Add your own structure (tension engine) and framing

Your video becomes more comprehensive than any single video already out there. That's a genuine value advantage, not plagiarism.

**Comment mining:**
> "Great video, but I really wished you covered how to stick with it long term."

That comment = your next section. Add it. Beat the competition on comprehensiveness.

## Checklist Before Publishing

- [ ] Hook opens a tension point in first 10 seconds
- [ ] Every section closes one loop and opens another
- [ ] No section longer than 90 seconds without a new tension point
- [ ] Final section leads naturally to pillar video
- [ ] Script covers everything top 3 competitor videos cover, plus comment requests
- [ ] Video is longer than top competitor videos (comprehensiveness signal)
