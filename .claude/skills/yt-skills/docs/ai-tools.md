# AI Tools

AI is flooding YouTube with cheap content. That's good news - not bad news - if you use it correctly.

Generic AI content is being commoditized. High-value, structured content using AI assistance is not. The gap between these two widens every month.

---

## The Commoditization Trap

Most creators using AI do this:
1. Ask AI to write a script on a topic
2. Generate stock footage video
3. Upload

The result: a generic video that looks and sounds like 10,000 other videos on the same topic. YouTube's algorithm is getting better at identifying this content and not pushing it.

The creators winning with AI do something different: they use AI to execute faster on a strategy that is inherently differentiated.

---

## What AI Commoditizes (Avoid These as Primary Strategy)

- Generic information videos with no unique angle
- Stock footage + AI voiceover on evergreen topics
- AI-generated entertainment content with no personality
- Trend-chasing content with no niche identity

These work short-term. They rarely compound into a real business.

---

## What AI Cannot Commoditize

- **Genuine expertise or documented experience** - your story, your results, your failures
- **Brand identity** - a consistent voice, visual style, and personality viewers recognize
- **Relationship with audience** - comments, community, direct engagement
- **Unique frameworks** - your named system or approach to solving the problem

If your channel has any of these, AI tools make you faster. Without them, AI tools make you generic.

## Using AI for Scripts

**The right process:**

1. Use combination method to identify topic (see [script-writing.md](./script-writing.md))
2. Feed AI the top 3 competitor video transcripts as context
3. Add comment requests you mined from those videos
4. Ask AI to write a script using the tension engine structure
5. Edit for your voice - remove anything that sounds generic

**What to give AI:**
- The topic and target audience
- Bullet points from competitor videos (your research)
- Comment requests you found
- Your tension engine structure template
- Any personal stories or examples you want included

**What AI gives back:**
- First draft structure
- Section transitions
- Tension point suggestions

**What you always add:**
- Your voice and personality
- Real examples from your niche
- The specific framing that matches your brand

## Using AI for Thumbnails

Tools like Tailsy generate thumbnail concepts from your title and topic.

**Process:**
1. Input your title and video topic
2. Generate 3–5 concepts
3. Pick the one with strongest curiosity gap
4. Add your face if applicable (face thumbnails outperform faceless in most niches)
5. Verify text reads at mobile thumbnail size (168×94px)

See [titles-thumbnails.md](./titles-thumbnails.md) for thumbnail checklist.

## Faceless Channel AI Stack

If you're building a faceless channel:

| Need | Tool Options |
|------|-------------|
| AI avatar/character | Nano Banana Pro, HeyGen, Synthesia |
| Script writing | Claude, ChatGPT (with tension engine template) |
| Voiceover | ElevenLabs, Play.ht |
| Thumbnail | Tailsy, Canva with AI, Adobe Firefly |
| Video editing | CapCut, Descript, Opus Clip |
| Research | Perplexity, Claude with web access |

**Faceless channel differentiation strategy:**
Create a consistent AI character - same voice, same visual style, same name. Channels doing this (health, finance, education niches) compound their brand even without a human face because viewers recognize the character.

## AI for Niche Research

AI accelerates the research phase from [niche-finding.md](./niche-finding.md):

**Prompts that work:**

```
"What are the most commonly expressed frustrations about [topic] 
on Reddit and forums? List specific phrases people use."
```

```
"For someone running a YouTube channel about [topic], 
what products or services does this audience typically buy? 
Give examples of affiliate programs in this space."
```

```
"List 10 YouTube video topics about [niche] that have performed 
well recently. For each, suggest a combination with another 
high-performing topic to create a new video concept."
```

Use AI output as a starting point - verify by actually checking Reddit, competitor channels, and affiliate marketplaces yourself.

## Staying Ahead of AI Commoditization

As AI tools improve, generic content becomes easier to make. The protection against being commoditized:

1. **Build brand IP now** - a personality, a named framework, a recognizable visual style
2. **Document real results** - your own journey, case studies, before/after
3. **Build a community** - audience relationships AI can't replicate
4. **Use AI to go deeper, not just faster** - use AI to add more value per video, not just produce more videos

The window where AI-assisted educational content stands out from pure AI-generated content is open now. The creators who build real brand identity during this window will be protected when it closes.
